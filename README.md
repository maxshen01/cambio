# Cambio

Cambio is a four-player card game with an in-memory Node.js/Socket.IO server and a React/Vite client. Lobby setup and memorization lead into playable clockwise deck-draw turns, with direct discard or replacement of an own-hand card. Discard-pile draws and active-game recovery are not available yet.

This README is the single maintained account of implementation capabilities, limitations, and coverage. Read it alongside the relevant source, tests, and design specifications before planning or implementing work. Source and tests establish behavior if this summary becomes stale.

## Current implementation

- **Lobby:** validated, normalized, unique display names; four stable seats; server-confirmed membership; Ready/Unready before dealing, including readiness before the room fills. Four connected, ready players trigger the deal.
- **Cards and memorization:** two shuffled decks including jokers, with four cards per player. Each recipient receives only their own bottom two opening faces. A separate Ready confirmation hides those faces and removes their IDs from client display state. Rejections preserve the authorized view. Departures during memorization clear the deal and readiness while retaining survivor seats, allowing a replacement and fresh deal.
- **First active turn:** after broadcasting the fourth memorization `playerReady`, the server uniformly selects one connected player, sets `phase: "active"` and `turnStep: "awaitingDraw"`, and broadcasts exactly one `turnChanged`. Randomness is injectable for tests and defaults to `Math.random`. Duplicate confirmations cannot start another turn; readiness is unavailable after activation.
- **Deck turns:** only the current player can draw once from the deck. The pending card stays outside their hand and only its owner receives its face ID. Direct discard puts it on the discard pile; replacement puts it facedown into an occupied own-hand slot and discards the displaced card. Both resolutions clear pending state, mark the discard top eligible, and advance once clockwise by stable seat order, wrapping seat four to seat one. Rejections preserve state and are requester-only.
- **Refill:** successful draws or discards retry refill when the deck has at most two cards and the discard has at least three. Older discards are shuffled into the deck bottom, preserving the remaining deck order and top two discard cards. Refill emits no extra public event or pile counts.
- **Board:** hands rotate around the local seat, all opening faces are removed on activation, and the current player receives a border and accessible turn marker. Setup readiness marks disappear during play. Ready Up and Call Cambio remain visible and disabled. The single shared area below the piles shows the drawn card upright, associated with its drawer. Deck, shared-card discard, and own-slot replacement controls follow eligibility and disable immediately while pending. Server events update the board; rejections restore eligible controls. Brief draw/discard/swap movements do not delay state processing and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- **Artwork:** public card images are preloaded, decoded, and cached; failed loads expose a retry action. Deck construction retains card identities and power metadata.
- **Connection and setup recovery:** initial connection failure can retry on the same socket while preserving the entered name. Established connection loss clears membership, pending requests, and private display state and requires reload; late events cannot restore the lost session. Unexpected lobby/setup handler faults are logged, reset the shared room while preserving its object identity, and disconnect all clients, including observers. The server remains listening. Expected domain errors remain requester-only and do not reset the room.

## Limitations and temporary behavior

- Discard-source drawing remains disabled, including when a visible discard top exists. Deck draws and their resolutions are the only supported turn actions.
- **Temporary power exception:** all powers are inactive. Every deck-drawn power card can be discarded immediately to end the turn or swapped into the drawer's hand. Neither the held nor displaced card triggers effects; no power event, prompt, or extra choice is sent. Card identity and power metadata are retained. This deliberately differs from the final power-resolution design.
- Matching, gifts, penalties, hand-limit additions, Call Cambio, final rounds, scoring, and results are not implemented. Reserved server-state fields do not establish working gameplay. Only implemented protocol branches are exposed in shared public types. Play has no normal round-ending flow yet.
- Active-game disconnect/abort recovery is not implemented: there is no survivor-preserving abort and redeal flow or `gameAborted` emission. Restart the server and reload clients to start a fresh session after reaching active play.
- Automatic reconnection/session resume is unsupported. State is not persisted. `gameState` is reserved and is not emitted.
- The final power-resolution rules remain the design target; temporary implementation exceptions belong here rather than in the design specifications.

## Running and validating

Use [Development](code/Development.md) for installation, environment setup, [local startup](code/Development.md#running-locally), [focused checks](code/Development.md#commands-and-checks), diagnostics, and the [pre-merge gate](code/Development.md#pre-merge-gate). See the [server](code/server/README.md) and [client](code/client/README.md) entry guides for package-specific links.

The current manual checkpoint is deck drawing through both resolutions: restart the server, reload and join four tabs, ready all players to deal, then confirm memorization separately. Draw from the deck and check that only the drawer sees its face. Click the shared card to discard it. On the next turn, draw again and click an occupied own-hand slot to replace it. Check the facedown replacement, displaced discard top, empty shared area, and exactly one clockwise advancement after each resolution. Observe the same behavior for a power card when encountered; deterministic power coverage is automated. Discard-pile drawing stays disabled. Active-game recovery is outside this checkpoint. Browser and visual acceptance remain user-performed; non-browser checks do not establish them. Individual check results and manual validation reports belong in chat or authorized GitHub updates.
